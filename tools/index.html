    Section · tools

    Clinical tools

    Interactive tools built on the same sourced knowledge graph as the rest of Anatomy Nexus: each result links back to the medicines, tests, anatomy and physiology it concerns. Educational, never a substitute for a clinician or pharmacist.

    Drug Interaction Checker flagship

    Add two or more medicines by generic name, brand, active ingredient or combination product and see what the official sources say about every pair: display severity with the state the source supports, why the interaction can occur, what to discuss with a clinician, what may be monitored, and the anatomy and tests involved. Never a bare "safe".

    Sourced records · every pair checked · severity from the cited wording

    Comparisons

    CRP vs ESR, creatinine vs eGFR, ECG vs echocardiogram, CT vs MRI, TSH vs free T4, HbA1c vs glucose, atorvastatin vs rosuvastatin: what each is for and how they differ.

    3D anatomy explorer

    Every one of 2,234 modelled pieces in 16 systems: search, isolate, x-ray, slice and explode the body, with the clinical topics of every structure on its card.

    Study modes

    Identify and locate structures in 3D, flashcards, quizzes over terminology, conditions, tests and medicines, and generated viva questions.

    Medical terms dictionary

    149 terms in 8 categories with pronunciation, plain meaning, examples, atlas links and the pages that use them.

    Search everything

    One search across structures, organs, systems, terms and every clinical topic, with results that link straight into the atlas.

    Planned

    Tools on the roadmap, listed so their absence is not a surprise. Nothing here is live yet.

    Result explanation tool planned

    Enter a test result and read the educational context of the biomarker: what it is, why it can be higher or lower and what can distort it. The biomarker pages already carry that content.

    BMI calculator and blood pressure guide planned

    Simple calculators tied to the physiology and health pages, with guideline thresholds cited to their source and country.

    Symptom–anatomy explorer planned

    Start from where it hurts and see the structures, systems and conditions of that region in 3D. The symptom pages already link each symptom to its anatomy.
