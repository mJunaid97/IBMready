Clinical tools

Interactive tools built on the same sourced knowledge graph as the rest of Anatomy Nexus: each result links back to the medicines, tests, anatomy and physiology it concerns. Educational, never a substitute for a clinician or pharmacist.

💊

Drug Interaction Checker flagship

Add two or more medicines by generic name, brand, active ingredient or combination product and see what the official sources say about every pair: display severity with the state the source supports, why the interaction can occur, what to discuss with a clinician, what may be monitored, and the anatomy and tests involved. Never a bare "safe".

Sourced records · every pair checked · severity from the cited wording

Comparisons

CRP vs ESR, creatinine vs eGFR, ECG vs echocardiogram, CT vs MRI, TSH vs free T4, HbA1c vs glucose, atorvastatin vs rosuvastatin: what each is for and how they differ.

Study modes
