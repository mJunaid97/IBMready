    Veins

    Systemic and pulmonary veins returning blood to the heart.

    Open in the 3D explorer Quiz me on this system Locate structures Flashcards

    Overview

    Veins return blood to the heart at low pressure, helped by valves and by the squeeze of surrounding muscles. Deep veins run alongside the arteries; superficial veins such as the cephalic, basilic and saphenous veins lie just under the skin. Blood from the gut drains first to the liver through the hepatic portal vein before joining the inferior vena cava. The pulmonary veins are the exception to the rule: they carry oxygenated blood from the lungs to the left atrium.

    Functions

    • Return blood to the heart
    • Act as a reservoir holding most of the blood volume
    • Route nutrient-rich blood from the gut through the liver (portal system)

    Clinical notes

    • Deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism
    • Varicose veins from failing valves in the superficial veins
    • Portal hypertension in liver disease
