    Urinary

    Kidneys, ureters, bladder and urethra.

    Open in the 3D explorer Quiz me on this system Locate structures Flashcards

    Overview

    The two kidneys filter about 180 litres of plasma a day, returning almost all of it to the blood and excreting the rest as urine. Urine drains through the ureters into the bladder, a muscular reservoir that empties through the urethra. The kidneys also regulate blood pressure, red cell production and vitamin D activation.

    Functions

    • Filter waste from the blood
    • Balance water, salts and acidity
    • Regulate blood pressure
    • Store and expel urine

    Clinical notes

    • Urinary tract infections
    • Kidney stones
    • Chronic kidney disease and dialysis
