    Teeth

    The permanent dentition: 32 teeth of the upper and lower jaws.

    Open in the 3D explorer Quiz me on this system Locate structures Flashcards

    Overview

    Adults have up to 32 permanent teeth: in each quadrant two incisors, one canine, two premolars and three molars. Incisors cut, canines tear, premolars and molars grind. Each tooth has a crown covered by enamel, the hardest tissue in the body, a root anchored in the jaw by the periodontal ligament, and a pulp cavity carrying vessels and nerves. This model includes 28 teeth; the third molars (wisdom teeth) are absent.

    Functions

    • Cut, tear and grind food to begin digestion
    • Support speech and the shape of the face

    Clinical notes

    • Dental caries dissolves enamel and dentine
    • Periodontal disease loosens the tooth's attachment
    • Impacted wisdom teeth are a common reason for extraction
