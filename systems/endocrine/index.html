    Endocrine

    Hormone-producing glands: thyroid, adrenal, pituitary, pineal.

    Open in the 3D explorer Quiz me on this system Locate structures Flashcards

    Overview

    Endocrine glands release hormones into the blood to regulate metabolism, growth, stress responses and reproduction. The pituitary, hanging from the base of the brain, controls most other glands. The adrenal glands cap the kidneys, producing cortisol, aldosterone and adrenaline. The pineal body, which sets the sleep-wake rhythm, is modelled with the brain; the thyroid gland is not included in this release.

    Functions

    • Coordinate metabolism, growth and stress responses
    • Regulate salt, water and blood pressure (adrenal cortex)
    • Control other glands (pituitary)

    Clinical notes

    • Diabetes mellitus
    • Thyroid over- and under-activity
    • Adrenal insufficiency and Cushing's syndrome
