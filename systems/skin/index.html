    Skin

    The integument: skin and hair.

    Open in the 3D explorer Quiz me on this system Locate structures Flashcards

    Overview

    The skin is the body's largest organ, about two square metres of barrier against infection, injury and water loss. Its outer epidermis constantly renews; the dermis beneath carries vessels, nerves, glands and hair follicles. In the atlas the skin is a single surface shown as a translucent overlay so the structures beneath stay visible; hair of the head, eyebrows and pubic hair are separate pieces.

    Functions

    • Protect against infection, injury and dehydration
    • Sense touch, pressure, temperature and pain
    • Regulate body temperature through sweat and blood flow
    • Make vitamin D

    Clinical notes

    • Eczema, psoriasis and acne
    • Skin cancers, including melanoma
    • Burns and wound healing
