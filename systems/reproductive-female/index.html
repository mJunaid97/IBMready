    Female reproductive

    The female pelvic organs and the breasts: uterus, cervix, vagina, uterine (fallopian) tubes, ovaries and their ligaments, the bladder, and the breast with its lobes, ducts and nipple, modelled from the NIH Human Reference Atlas and fitted into the reference body.

    Open in the 3D explorer Quiz me on this system Locate structures Flashcards

    Overview

    This layer brings the female body into an atlas whose reference model is male. Its pieces come from the united female model of the Human Reference Atlas (HRA), the reference built by the HuBMAP Consortium and the NIH from the Visible Human Female, scaled and placed so that the pubic symphysis, sacral promontory and hip bones of the female pelvis coincide with those of the BodyParts3D pelvis; the breasts sit on the reference chest wall at the level of the fourth to fifth rib. The uterus lies behind and above the bladder and in front of the rectum, tilted forward; the cervix opens into the top of the vagina, which runs down and back to the perineum; the uterine tubes (fallopian tubes) arch from the upper corners of the uterus out over the ovaries, which lie against the side walls of the pelvis; and the broad, round, cardinal and uterosacral ligaments and the suspensory and ovarian ligaments hold everything in place. The female bladder from the same model, with its trigone and neck, replaces the male bladder while the layer is shown, and a short schematic urethra runs from its neck down the front of the vagina to the vestibule (the HRA model has no urethra mesh). Each breast is shown as its body (fat and gland), the lobes of the mammary gland, the lactiferous ducts and sinuses, the suspensory ligaments, the areola with its tubercles and the nipple. Two things to know when reading it. The Visible Human Female was a woman of 59, and the whole layer is scaled to the male pelvis, so the uterus and ovaries are those of a woman past the menopause and smaller than the textbook figures on the organ pages. And because the reference skin is male, the breasts stand outside the skin overlay when both are shown. Switching this layer on hides the male reproductive organs, bladder and urethra so the two bodies are not drawn through each other; switching it off brings them back. The Female body preset shows the whole body with the female organs. The vulva and clitoris are not part of the HRA model and remain a written page.

    Functions

    • Produce eggs and the hormones oestrogen and progesterone (ovaries)
    • Catch and carry the egg and host fertilisation (uterine tubes)
    • Shed the lining each month or carry a pregnancy and deliver it (uterus and cervix)
    • Form the birth canal and the passage for menstrual blood (vagina)
    • Store and pass urine (bladder and urethra)
    • Make and deliver milk (breasts)

    Clinical notes

    • Endometriosis, fibroids, adenomyosis and heavy periods
    • Polycystic ovary syndrome and ovarian cysts
    • Cervical screening and HPV vaccination prevent most cervical cancer; ovarian cancer presents late
    • Ectopic pregnancy in the uterine tube is an emergency
    • Pelvic organ prolapse and urinary incontinence after childbirth and the menopause
    • Breast cancer is the commonest cancer in women; screening mammography and awareness of breast change find it early

    Organs & groups in this system

    Uterus & cervix

    The womb: a pear-sized muscular organ behind the bladder whose lining is shed each month or supports a pregnancy; the cervix is its neck, opening into the vagina.

    6 structures · full anatomy article

    Ovaries & fallopian tubes

    The female gonads, one on each side of the uterus, release an egg each cycle and make oestrogen and progesterone; the fallopian tubes carry the egg to the uterus and are where fertilisation happens.

    10 structures · full anatomy article

    Vagina

    A muscular, elastic canal about 8 to 10 cm long from the cervix to the vulva, between the bladder and urethra in front and the rectum behind: the birth canal and the passage for menstrual blood.

    1 structures · full anatomy article

    Ligaments of the uterus & ovary

    The peritoneal folds and fibromuscular bands that suspend the uterus, tubes and ovaries: the broad ligament with its mesosalpinx and mesovarium, the round, cardinal and uterosacral ligaments, the suspensory and ovarian ligaments and the vesico-uterine pouch.

    18 structures

    Vulva & clitoris

    The external female genitalia: the mons pubis, labia majora and minora, the clitoris, the vestibule with the urethral and vaginal openings, and the glands that open into it.

    not modelled in 3D · full anatomy article

    Breasts

    Modified sweat glands on the front of the chest wall over pectoralis major: 15 to 20 lobes of milk-producing tissue draining to the nipple, in fat and supporting ligaments, with lymph drainage mainly to the armpit.
