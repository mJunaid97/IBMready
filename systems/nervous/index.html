    Nervous system

    Brain, spinal cord, cranial and spinal nerves, ganglia and meninges.

    Open in the 3D explorer Quiz me on this system Locate structures Flashcards

    Overview

    The nervous system gathers information, decides and commands. The brain and spinal cord form the central nervous system; the cranial and spinal nerves with their ganglia form the peripheral nervous system. This model is richest in the head: the cerebral gyri and lobes, the basal ganglia, thalamus and hypothalamus, the brainstem and cerebellum, the ventricles, the optic pathway and the nerves of the orbit. Peripheral nerves of the limbs are not part of this BodyParts3D release.

    Functions

    • Sense the environment and the body's internal state
    • Integrate information, memory and emotion
    • Control voluntary and involuntary movement
    • Regulate organs through the autonomic system

    Clinical notes

    • Stroke: loss of blood supply to part of the brain
    • Epilepsy, migraine and neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson's
    • Nerve compression syndromes and neuropathies

    Organs & groups in this system

    Brain

    The cerebrum, cerebellum and brainstem: about 1.4 kg of tissue that controls thought, movement, sensation and every organ.
