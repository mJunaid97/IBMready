Educational use only

Anatomy Nexus is an educational resource about the human body and medicine. Nothing on this site is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and nothing here creates a clinician–patient relationship. Content is general: it cannot take account of your history, examination, test results or circumstances.

Emergencies

If you or someone else may be seriously ill or injured, call your local emergency number now (999 in the UK, 112 in Europe, 911 in North America). First-aid pages follow published resuscitation and first-aid guidance but do not replace a certified first-aid course or the instructions of the emergency call handler.

Medicines and tests

Medication pages describe how medicines work and what to watch for; they are not prescribing information. Never start, stop or change a medicine on the basis of this site. Test pages explain what tests measure; reference ranges differ between laboratories and results are only meaningful when a clinician reads them in context.

Content has not yet been independently reviewed by a clinician (see the medical review policy).
