    Medical tests

    Medical tests: what each measures, why it is ordered, how it is done, how results are read and what a test cannot tell you. Panels are broken down into their components, each linked to the biomarker it measures. Reference ranges belong to the laboratory that ran the test; guideline thresholds are quoted with their source and country. Written for education, not for interpreting an individual's results.
