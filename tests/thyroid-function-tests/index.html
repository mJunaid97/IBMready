    Thyroid function tests

    Also known as: TFTs, TSH, free T4, thyroid blood test

    TSH from the pituitary and the thyroid hormones T4 and T3 measured together. Because the pituitary reacts to tiny changes in hormone level, TSH is the most sensitive single indicator of thyroid status.

    Open in the 3D explorerPituitary gland anatomy

    What it measures

    MeasureWhat it tells you
    TSHThyroid-stimulating hormone; high when the thyroid is underactive, suppressed when it is overactive.
    Free T4The main hormone the thyroid releases; low in hypothyroidism, high in hyperthyroidism.
    Free T3The active hormone; added when TSH is low to detect T3 toxicosis.
    Thyroid antibodiesTPO antibodies mark autoimmune (Hashimoto) thyroiditis; TSH-receptor antibodies mark Graves disease.

    Why it is ordered

    • Tiredness, weight change, feeling cold or hot, palpitations, anxiety
    • A goitre or neck lump
    • Atrial fibrillation, high cholesterol, depression
    • Monitoring levothyroxine or antithyroid drugs
    • Amiodarone or lithium treatment, pregnancy planning

    How it is done

    Venous blood, any time of day (take levothyroxine after the sample).

    Preparation. None; biotin supplements can interfere and should be paused for two days.

    Reading the result

    FindingUsual meaning
    High TSH, low T4Primary hypothyroidism
    High TSH, normal T4Subclinical hypothyroidism; treat if symptomatic, pregnant or TSH very high
    Low TSH, high T4 or T3Hyperthyroidism: Graves disease, toxic nodules, thyroiditis
    Low TSH and low T4Pituitary (secondary) failure, or recent overtreatment

    Reference ranges vary by laboratory, method, age, sex and clinical context; a result is read against the person, not a table.

    Limitations

    Acute illness and some drugs disturb results ('sick euthyroid'); repeat once recovered. TSH lags six weeks behind a dose change.

    Educational content. Reference ranges differ between laboratories, methods, ages and sexes, and results are always read against the person, the question asked and other findings. Educational only.
