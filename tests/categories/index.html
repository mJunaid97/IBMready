    Medical test categories

    Every kind of medical test in 36 categories: laboratory, imaging, specialty diagnostics, measurements, screening tools and procedures, with the tests that have a page and the catalogued concepts still to be written.

    A test can belong to several categories but has one canonical page. A catalogued concept is a clinical idea the site knows (with its abbreviations and synonyms) and maps terminology onto; it becomes a page only when it can be written to the site's quality standard, never as a thin placeholder.

    Laboratory 21

    Tests on blood, urine, stool, swabs, tissue and other body fluids.

    Blood & haematology

    Counts and appearance of the cells in blood, haemoglobin and its variants, red-cell survival and the bone marrow that makes them.

    5 pages · 41 catalogued concepts

    Coagulation & haemostasis

    How well blood clots and how quickly clots are broken down: screening times, individual factors, platelet function and the inherited or acquired tendencies to bleed or clot.

    1 page · 34 catalogued concepts

    Chemistry & metabolic

    The salts, gases, proteins, enzymes, waste products and fuels measured in serum or plasma: the core chemistry of every hospital laboratory.

    7 pages · 47 catalogued concepts

    Kidney & urinary

    Blood and urine measurements of how well the kidneys filter, concentrate and conserve, and the dipstick and microscopy examination of urine.

    6 pages · 42 catalogued concepts

    Liver & hepatobiliary

    Enzymes, proteins and clotting factors that reflect liver-cell damage, bile flow and synthetic function, plus the antibody, viral, genetic and imaging tests of liver disease.

    4 pages · 21 catalogued concepts

    Lipid & cardiometabolic

    Cholesterol, triglycerides and the lipoproteins that carry them, with the newer markers used to refine cardiovascular risk.

    2 pages · 17 catalogued concepts

    Diabetes & glucose

    Glucose now, glucose over the last months, the insulin the pancreas makes and the antibodies that mark autoimmune diabetes.

    3 pages · 21 catalogued concepts

    Endocrine & hormone

    Hormones of the thyroid, adrenal, pituitary and parathyroid glands and the dynamic tests that provoke or suppress them.

    4 pages · 41 catalogued concepts

    Reproductive & fertility

    Sex hormones, ovarian reserve, semen analysis and the tests of ovulation used in fertility assessment.

    2 pages · 23 catalogued concepts

    Pregnancy & prenatal

    Confirming a pregnancy and, once established, the screening, diagnostic and monitoring tests of mother and fetus.

    2 pages · 22 catalogued concepts

    Vitamins, minerals & nutrition

    Iron and its carriers, the B vitamins, fat-soluble vitamins and trace elements: the deficiencies and excesses that show in blood.

    4 pages · 23 catalogued concepts

    Immunology & inflammation

    Markers of inflammation and infection, the complement system and the immunoglobulins that make up humoral immunity.

    3 pages · 14 catalogued concepts

    Autoimmune & rheumatology

    The autoantibodies and genetic markers used in lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, vasculitis, coeliac disease and autoimmune liver disease.

    0 pages · 26 catalogued concepts

    Allergy

    Blood, skin and challenge tests that identify what a person reacts to and how severely.

    0 pages · 10 catalogued concepts

    Infectious disease & microbiology

    Finding the organism: cultures, microscopy, antigen and antibody tests, PCR and sequencing for bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites, and testing which drugs will work.

    5 pages · 77 catalogued concepts

    Genetic & molecular

    Tests of chromosomes, genes and variants, from a karyotype to whole-genome sequencing, and their use in inherited disease, cancer and drug response.

    1 page · 30 catalogued concepts

    Cancer & tumour markers

    Proteins and genetic changes measured in blood or tissue to help evaluate, stage, guide and monitor cancer. A marker alone rarely diagnoses cancer.

    0 pages · 37 catalogued concepts

    Toxicology & drug monitoring

    Measuring medicines with a narrow safety margin, screening for drugs of misuse and detecting poisons, gases and heavy metals.

    0 pages · 33 catalogued concepts

    Pathology & cytology

    Tissue and cells examined under the microscope: biopsies, smears, aspirates, special stains and the molecular tests done on them.

    2 pages · 15 catalogued concepts

    Blood bank & transfusion

    Grouping, antibody screening and crossmatching so that transfused blood is compatible, and the tests of a suspected transfusion reaction.

    0 pages · 11 catalogued concepts

    Body fluid analysis

    What is measured in cerebrospinal, pleural, ascitic, joint, pericardial and other fluids drawn from the body.

    1 page · 22 catalogued concepts

    Imaging 1

    Pictures of the inside of the body.

    Imaging

    X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI, nuclear medicine and bone densitometry: every way of making a picture of the inside of the body.

    9 pages · 68 catalogued concepts

    Specialty diagnostics 10

    Recordings, functional tests, endoscopy and specialist examinations, organised by body system.

    Cardiovascular

    Recordings, ultrasound, stress, catheter and vascular tests of the heart and blood vessels.

    10 pages · 37 catalogued concepts

    Respiratory

    How much air the lungs move, how well gas crosses into blood, and the challenge, exercise, sampling and endoscopic tests of the airways and lungs.

    4 pages · 20 catalogued concepts

    Neurology

    Recordings of the brain, nerves and muscles, tests of the spinal fluid and autonomic function, cognitive assessment and the imaging of the nervous system.

    3 pages · 21 catalogued concepts

    Gastroenterology

    Endoscopy, motility and acid studies, breath tests, liver assessment and the imaging of the digestive tract.

    8 pages · 32 catalogued concepts

    Kidney & urology diagnostics

    Imaging, endoscopy, pressure studies and biopsy of the kidneys, bladder and prostate, alongside the urine and blood tests of kidney function.

    8 pages · 16 catalogued concepts

    Eye & ophthalmic

    Tests of how well the eyes see and the examinations and imaging of every part of the eye.

    0 pages · 18 catalogued concepts

    Hearing & balance

    Hearing thresholds, middle-ear and inner-ear function, and the tests that find the cause of dizziness and vertigo.

    0 pages · 14 catalogued concepts

    Sleep

    Studies that record breathing, oxygen, brain activity and movement during sleep, and daytime tests of sleepiness.

    0 pages · 7 catalogued concepts

    Dermatology

    Looking at, sampling and testing the skin: dermoscopy, biopsy, allergy patch tests, fungal tests and light-based examinations.

    1 page · 9 catalogued concepts

    Bone, joint & musculoskeletal

    Imaging of bones and joints, bone density, joint fluid, nerve and muscle studies and the blood tests of inflammatory and autoimmune joint disease.

    8 pages · 14 catalogued concepts

    Measurements, screening & procedures 4

    Vital signs, validated assessment tools, diagnostic procedures and tests done at the bedside or at home.

    Vital signs & physiological measurements

    The measurements taken at every consultation and hospital observation round, and what each reflects in the body.

    3 pages · 13 catalogued concepts

    Screening & clinical assessment

    Validated questionnaires and scores used to screen, grade severity and estimate risk. Proprietary instruments are described, never reproduced.

    1 page · 18 catalogued concepts

    Endoscopy & diagnostic procedures

    Procedures done to look inside the body or take a sample: scopes of the airways, gut, bladder and joints, catheter studies, punctures and biopsies.

    7 pages · 22 catalogued concepts

    Point-of-care & home testing

    Tests done at the bedside, in a pharmacy or at home, with a meter, a strip or a self-collected sample.
