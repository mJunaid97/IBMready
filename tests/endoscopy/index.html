    Endoscopy (as a test)

    Also known as: scope test, gastroscopy, colonoscopy, bronchoscopy, cystoscopy, arthroscopy

    A flexible or rigid tube with a camera passed into a body cavity through a natural opening or a small cut, so the lining can be seen directly and sampled. Each region has its own scope: the gullet and stomach (gastroscopy), the large bowel (colonoscopy), the airways (bronchoscopy), the bladder (cystoscopy) and joints (arthroscopy).

    Open in the 3D explorerOesophagus anatomy

    What it measures

    MeasureWhat it tells you
    Direct view of the liningInflammation, ulcers, bleeding, polyps and tumours are seen and photographed.
    BiopsiesTiny samples taken painlessly from the lining for microscopy.
    TherapyPolyps removed, bleeding stopped, narrowings stretched, stones retrieved at the same sitting.

    Why it is ordered

    • Difficulty swallowing, persistent indigestion, vomiting blood, weight loss (gastroscopy)
    • Bleeding from the bowel, change in bowel habit, positive stool screening test, anaemia (colonoscopy)
    • Coughing blood, a shadow on a chest scan (bronchoscopy)
    • Blood in the urine (cystoscopy)
    • Surveillance of Barrett oesophagus, ulcerative colitis, polyps

    How it is done

    Usually a day case with throat spray or sedation; 10-45 minutes depending on the scope. Full details are on each procedure page.

    Preparation. Fasting for upper scopes; bowel preparation laxatives for colonoscopy; stop blood thinners as instructed.

    Reading the result

    FindingUsual meaning
    Ulcer with visible vesselHigh re-bleeding risk; treated endoscopically
    Polyp removed, adenoma on histologySurveillance interval set by number and size
    Mass biopsied, malignantStaging scans and multidisciplinary team planning

    Reference ranges vary by laboratory, method, age, sex and clinical context; a result is read against the person, not a table.

    Limitations

    Invasive with small risks of bleeding and perforation; the small bowel is mostly out of reach (capsule endoscopy covers it).

    Educational content. Reference ranges differ between laboratories, methods, ages and sexes, and results are always read against the person, the question asked and other findings. This page cannot interpret an individual result.
