Study

Learning anatomy and medicine is mostly retrieval practice. Pick a scope, let the atlas highlight or name a structure, and answer; then test the clinical knowledge that hangs off it. Scores stay on your device.

Identify the structure in 3D

Each question highlights one structure in the atlas and offers four names from the same system. Keys 1–4 answer.

Locate the structure in 3D

The reverse exercise, and the one exams use: the atlas names a structure and you click it on the body. Great for surface anatomy and for learning where things sit.

By region

Terminology and clinical quizzes

Match a definition to its name. Ten questions per round; pick a bank.

Viva practice

Short-answer questions of the kind asked in an oral exam, generated from the physiology key facts, conditions and first-aid steps. Think of the answer, then reveal it.

Flashcards

The atlas shows a structure; reveal its name and a one-line description, then move on.
