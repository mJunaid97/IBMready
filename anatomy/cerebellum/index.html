    Cerebellum anatomy

    The 'little brain' behind the brainstem coordinates balance, posture and the timing of movements.

    Open in the 3D explorer Locate structures Quiz: nervous system
    Overview page. A full anatomy article for the cerebellum has not been written yet; below are the modelled structures, the system it belongs to and every clinical topic that mentions it.

    About

    The 'little brain' behind the brainstem, with two hemispheres and a central vermis.

    Function. Coordinates balance, posture and the timing and smoothness of movement; also contributes to learning and language.

    Clinical note. Damage causes unsteady gait, tremor and slurred speech; alcohol acts on it acutely.

    The nervous system in brief

    The nervous system gathers information, decides and commands. The brain and spinal cord form the central nervous system; the cranial and spinal nerves with their ganglia form the peripheral nervous system. This model is richest in the head: the cerebral gyri and lobes, the basal ganglia, thalamus and hypothalamus, the brainstem and cerebellum, the ventricles, the optic pathway and the nerves of the orbit. Peripheral nerves of the limbs are not part of this BodyParts3D release.
