    Ear anatomy

    Also known as: ears, hearing

    The external ear gathers sound; the middle and inner ear (not modelled here) convert it to nerve signals and sense balance.

    Open in the 3D explorer Locate structures Quiz: sensory organs
    Overview page. A full anatomy article for the ear has not been written yet; below are the modelled structures, the system it belongs to and every clinical topic that mentions it.

    About

    The auricle (pinna): the visible ear of skin-covered elastic cartilage.

    Function. Collects sound and funnels it into the ear canal; its shape helps locate sounds.

    The sensory organs in brief

    The eye focuses light through the cornea and lens onto the retina, whose signals travel along the optic nerve to the brain. The model separates the cornea, sclera, choroid, iris, lens, vitreous body and retina, plus the lacrimal apparatus that produces and drains tears and the tarsal plates of the eyelids. The external ear collects sound towards the tympanic membrane; the middle and inner ear are not modelled in this release.
