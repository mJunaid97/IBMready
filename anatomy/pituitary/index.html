    Pituitary gland anatomy

    Also known as: hypophysis

    The master gland at the base of the brain, controlling growth, thyroid, adrenal and reproductive hormones.

    About

    A pea-sized gland hanging from the hypothalamus in a bony hollow of the sphenoid.

    Function. The 'master gland': controls growth, thyroid, adrenal and reproductive function and water balance.

    Clinical note. Pituitary tumours can press on the optic chiasm, narrowing the visual fields.

    The endocrine in brief

    Endocrine glands release hormones into the blood to regulate metabolism, growth, stress responses and reproduction. The pituitary, hanging from the base of the brain, controls most other glands. The adrenal glands cap the kidneys, producing cortisol, aldosterone and adrenaline. The pineal body, which sets the sleep-wake rhythm, is modelled with the brain; the thyroid gland is not included in this release.
