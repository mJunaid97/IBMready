    The reproductive in brief

    BodyParts3D is built on an adult male, so this layer contains the male reproductive organs: the testes, which produce sperm and testosterone, the epididymis where sperm mature, the deferent ducts that carry them to the urethra, the seminal vesicles and prostate that add most of the seminal fluid, and the erectile bodies of the penis.
