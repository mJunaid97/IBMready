    Roadmap

    Anatomy Nexus: a comprehensive, visual, interconnected human-body knowledge platform. Anatomy is the foundation; every other section connects back to it so a reader can move from body part → structure → function → symptoms → conditions → tests → procedures → treatment → learning.

    Phases

    Phase 1 · Foundation live

    Human anatomy and the data structure everything else connects to.

    • 3D anatomy explorer (2,234 pieces, 16 systems) live
    • Entity data model: structures, systems, organs, regions, terms live
    • Body system pages live
    • Organ pages with live 3D live
    • Women's anatomy (uterus, ovaries, vagina, vulva, breasts, pelvic floor) and the anatomy of gender-affirming surgery (neovagina, neophallus) live
    • Deep links and embeds for every structure, organ, region and system live

    Phase 2 · High-value educational layer live

    Natural extensions of the anatomy content.

    • Medical terminology dictionary live
    • Student quizzes (identify the structure, terminology) live
    • Flashcards live
    • Physiology topics per system live
    • Histology, pathology and embryology primers planned

    Phase 3 · Medical knowledge live

    From anatomy into real-world medical knowledge.

    • Diseases & conditions database live
    • Symptoms explorer (educational, not diagnostic) live
    • Medical tests library live
    • Medical imaging library live
    • Women's health: menstrual cycle, pregnancy, menopause, endometriosis, PCOS, fibroids, breast, cervical and ovarian cancer, prolapse, incontinence, cervical screening live

    Phase 4 · Clinical understanding live

    Procedures, medications and clinical terminology. Advanced pathology primers remain planned.

    • Medical procedures live
    • Medication information (educational) live
    • Clinical terminology live
    • Gender-affirming care: hormone therapy physiology, vaginoplasty, phalloplasty and metoidioplasty, chest masculinisation, estradiol and testosterone live

    Phase 5 · Public health & practical knowledge live

    First aid, emergencies, prevention and lifestyle.

    • First aid & emergency education live
    • Health & prevention: exercise, sleep, nutrition, smoking, alcohol, hydration live

    Phase 6 · Advanced interactive platform started

    Knowledge graph, personalised learning, image-based learning and clinical cases.

    • Knowledge graph across all entities live
    • Clinical layer: structured tests, medicines, interactions and comparisons live
    • Locate-the-structure exercises and viva practice live
    • Progress tracking and personalised learning planned
    • Clinical case studies planned

    Phase 7 · Comprehensive tests & medications started

    Terminology-driven expansion: canonical identifiers, controlled vocabularies, taxonomies and ingestion pipelines so any clinically meaningful test or medicine that maps to an authoritative source can be represented without thin pages.

    • Master test taxonomy (36 categories, 837 catalogued concepts) live
    • Medication taxonomy (31 therapeutic areas, 536 classes with ATC codes) live
    • Controlled vocabularies, drug-name rules and quality gates in the compiler live
    • LOINC and RxNorm ingestion pipelines with verification of asserted codes live
    • Editorial review queues and provenance metadata live
    • Phase 2–3 seed coverage: coagulation, iron, vitamins, hepatitis, HIV, blood pressure, DXA; antiplatelets, beta blockers, SSRIs, macrolides, opioids, antihistamines, inhaled steroids, a vaccine, a contrast agent, a radiopharmaceutical live
    • Terminology-driven expansion from LOINC and RxNorm releases (Phase 4 of the specification) planned
    • Independent medical review of every clinical page planned

    Sections

    Physiology live

    Anatomy explains what is there; physiology explains what it does. Cardiovascular, respiratory, nervous, digestive, renal and endocrine physiology topics will attach to the system and organ pages.

    Learn planned

    Histology, pathology, embryology and clinical terminology primers.

    Symptoms explorer live

    Start from what a person experiences (headache, chest pain, knee pain…). Each page: what it is, relevant anatomy, common and less common causes, associated symptoms, when to seek urgent care, related conditions, tests and anatomy. The site educates; it does not diagnose.

    Diseases & conditions live

    Structured condition pages: definition, affected body part and system, causes, risk factors, symptoms, complications, diagnosis, imaging, treatment, procedures, prevention and references, all linked to anatomy.

    Medical tests live

    Blood, urine, heart, lung and other tests: what they measure, how they are done, what results generally indicate, and which anatomy they relate to.

    Medical imaging live

    X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, PET, mammography and fluoroscopy: how each works, what it shows, how normal anatomy appears and common abnormalities.

    Medical procedures live

    Appendectomy, knee replacement, angioplasty, endoscopy and more, explained step by step with the anatomy involved.

    Medications live

    Educational medication pages: class, mechanism, target organ or system, side effects, warnings and related conditions.

    First aid & emergency knowledge live

    CPR, choking, bleeding, burns, fractures, fainting, seizures and more, aligned with authoritative guidance.

    Health & prevention live

    How exercise, sleep, nutrition, smoking, alcohol and hydration act on the organs and systems described in the atlas.

    Student learning platform live

    Flashcards, quizzes, anatomy identification, labeling exercises, viva practice and exam modes for medicine, nursing, physiotherapy, dentistry and allied health.

    Knowledge graph live

    Every entity carries typed links so a reader can keep exploring: Heart → cardiovascular system → cardiac physiology → coronary arteries → ECG → heart failure → angioplasty → medications → symptoms → tests.

    Clinical layer live

    Tests, biomarkers, medicines, drug targets, interactions and comparisons as structured, source-backed, region-aware entities: every warning, contraindication, indication, threshold and interaction cites the official document and country it came from, and every page shows its review status.

    What comes next planned

    Histology, embryology and pathology primers; more conditions, tests and medications on the same schema; image-based learning (labelled radiographs and slides); clinical case studies; progress tracking and personalised revision; a female reference body when an open dataset allows.

    Entity model

    Reusable entities with explicit relationships, so no page is an island. Details in ARCHITECTURE.md.

    AnatomicalStructure live
    1,671 named structures over 2,234 mesh pieces; FMA concept id, system, side, pair, parents, region tags, organ membership.
    data/hd/atlas.json → structures; descriptions in data/content/atlas-content.json → structures
    BodySystem live
    16 systems with overview, functions, clinical notes, related systems, key structures; the reproductive system covers female, male and gender-affirming anatomy.
    content/systems.json
    Organ live
    47 organs and skeletal groups: 40 resolved to atlas structures by rule, and 7 (uterus and cervix, ovaries and fallopian tubes, vagina, vulva and clitoris, breasts, neovagina, neophallus) that the adult male reference body does not model, published as full written anatomy pages that show the modelled structures around them; aliases for search.
    content/organs.json
    Region live
    7 spatial regions computed from bounding boxes.
    content/regions.json
    MedicalTerm live
    149 terms in 8 categories (directions, planes, movements, regions, structures, word parts, disease processes, clinical terms) with atlas links and pronunciation.
    content/terms.json
    Quiz / Flashcard live
    Identify-the-structure, locate-the-structure and flashcards in 3D; terminology, condition, test and medication quizzes; viva questions from physiology key facts.
    generated at runtime from structures and terms
    PhysiologyTopic live
    30 entries. Links: explains → BodySystem / Organ / AnatomicalStructure.
    content/physiology.json → data/content/types/*.json
    Symptom live
    18 entries. Links: involves → AnatomicalStructure / Organ; suggests → Condition; evaluatedBy → MedicalTest.
    content/symptoms.json → data/content/types/*.json
    Condition live
    40 entries. Links: affects → Organ / BodySystem; causes → Symptom; diagnosedBy → MedicalTest / ImagingStudy; treatedBy → Procedure / Medication.
    content/conditions.json → data/content/types/*.json
    MedicalTest live
    38 entries. Links: measures → Biomarker; evaluates → Condition; monitors → Condition / Medication. v1.2: full-depth pages. v1.4: kind, 36-category taxonomy, specimens, methods, LOINC codes, panels ↔ components, catalogue coverage.
    content/tests.json → data/content/types/*.json
    ImagingStudy live
    7 entries. Links: shows → Region / Organ; usedFor → Condition.
    content/imaging.json → data/content/types/*.json
    Procedure live
    14 entries. Links: operatesOn → Organ / AnatomicalStructure; treats → Condition.
    content/procedures.json → data/content/types/*.json
    Medication live
    39 entries. Links: targets → Organ / BodySystem; treats → Condition. v1.2: full-depth pages. v1.4: product type, therapeutic areas, vocabulary routes and dosage forms, ingredient variants, availability by country, RxNorm / ATC / FDA EPC codes; vaccines, contrast agents and radiopharmaceuticals as first-class entities.
    content/medications.json → data/content/types/*.json
    FirstAidTopic / HealthTopic live
    16 + 7 entries. Links: concerns → Organ / BodySystem.
    content/first-aid.json, content/health.json → data/content/types/*.json
    Biomarker live
    30 analytes measured by the tests: what each is, which organ makes or clears it, why higher or lower, what distorts it; linked from every test component and from the medicines that change it.
    content/biomarkers.json → /biomarkers/
    BiologicalTarget live
    22 receptors, enzymes, ion channels, transporters and pathways with the medicines and classes that act on them: the bridge from a medicine to the anatomy it changes.
    content/targets.json → /targets/
    DrugInteraction live
    72 sourced pair records (drug–drug, drug–class, therapeutic duplication) with mechanism, effect, official wording, management, monitoring and a severity state assigned only where the cited wording supports it.
    content/interactions.json → medication and class pages, /tools/drug-interaction-checker/
    MedicationProduct live
    14 brand and combination products mapped to their active ingredients so a brand resolves to its generic page and a combination is checked ingredient by ingredient.
    content/products.json → search, /tools/drug-interaction-checker/
    Comparison live
    7 structured comparisons (CRP vs ESR, creatinine vs eGFR, ECG vs echocardiogram, CT vs MRI, TSH vs free T4, HbA1c vs glucose, atorvastatin vs rosuvastatin) drawn from the entity pages' own properties.
    content/comparisons.json → /compare/
    KnowledgeGraph live
    3,247 validated typed links between 182 topics, 38 organs, 15 systems, 195 structures and 149 terms; every reverse link is precomputed so any page can show what points at it.
    tools/build-content.py → data/content/knowledge.json, clinical.json, search-index.json
    TestCategory / TestConcept live
    36 categories in four groups over 837 catalogued test concepts (abbreviations, synonyms, kind); concepts resolve to pages or stay database-only, never thin pages.
    content/test-taxonomy.json → data/content/test-categories.json
    TherapeuticArea / ClassConcept live
    31 therapeutic areas → 536 pharmacologic class concepts with WHO ATC codes and links to the drug-class pages.
    content/medication-taxonomy.json → data/content/medication-taxonomy.json
    Vocabulary term live
    Controlled vocabularies for test kinds, specimens, methods, modalities, routes, dosage forms, regulatory statuses, product types; values outside them fail the build.
    content/vocabularies.json → data/content/vocabularies.json
