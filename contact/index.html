    Contact

    Report an error, suggest a topic, ask about reuse, or offer to review pages.

    Report an error or suggest an improvement

    The fastest route is the public issue tracker of the project repository, where every report is visible and tracked until it is resolved:

    Open the issue tracker

    Please include the page address, what is wrong, and a source that supports the correction if you have one. See the corrections policy for what happens next.

    Clinicians and educators

    If you would like to review pages in your specialty, contribute content, or use the atlas in teaching, please open an issue with a short note about your background; we will reply there.

    Reuse

    The 3D geometry is derived from BodyParts3D (CC BY 4.0). Text on this site may be quoted with attribution and a link to the page. For anything beyond that, ask first.

    Health questions

    We cannot answer questions about your own health or anyone else's. Please contact a clinician, a pharmacist or your local health service; in an emergency, call your emergency number.
