    Pulmonary embolism

    Also known as: PE, blood clot in the lung

    Blockage of a pulmonary artery by a clot that has travelled from a vein, usually in the leg or pelvis.

    Open in the 3D explorerLungs anatomy

    Overview

    A pulmonary embolism cuts off blood flow to part of the lung: the affected segment is ventilated but not perfused, oxygen levels fall and the right ventricle strains against the obstruction. Small emboli cause pleuritic pain and breathlessness; a large one can cause collapse and death within minutes.

    Causes

    • Deep vein thrombosis of the leg or pelvis
    • Rarely fat, air or amniotic fluid emboli

    Risk factors

    • Recent surgery or immobility
    • Cancer
    • Pregnancy and the postpartum period
    • Oestrogen-containing medicines
    • Previous clots or inherited thrombophilia
    • Obesity, smoking, long-haul travel

    Symptoms

    Shortness of breathChest painCoughDizzinessSwelling (oedema)

    What a clinician may find

    • Fast breathing and pulse
    • Low oxygen saturation
    • Signs of DVT in a leg
    • Low blood pressure in massive PE

    Complications

    • Collapse and cardiac arrest
    • Right heart failure
    • Chronic pulmonary hypertension

    Diagnosis

    Probability scoring and D-dimer, then CT pulmonary angiography to show the clot; ventilation-perfusion scanning in pregnancy or kidney disease. ECG and echocardiography assess strain on the right heart.

    Pulse oximetry and blood gasesElectrocardiogramTroponin testFull blood count
    CT scanUltrasoundX-ray

    Treatment

    Immediate anticoagulation (low-molecular-weight heparin, then a direct oral anticoagulant or warfarin for three to six months or longer); clot-dissolving drugs or catheter removal for massive PE with shock; oxygen and support.

    Apixaban and DOACsWarfarin

    Prevention

    DVT prevention: early mobilisation, prophylactic injections in hospital, compression stockings, keeping moving on long journeys.

    When to seek care

    Emergency services for sudden breathlessness, chest pain on breathing, coughing blood or collapse, especially with a swollen leg or recent surgery.
