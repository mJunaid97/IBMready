    Organs by body system

    39 organs and skeletal groups assembled from the pieces of the 3D atlas, grouped by the system they belong to. Each opens its anatomy page: structure, function, a live 3D model and every clinical topic that concerns it.

    Skeleton

    Skull

    22 bones forming the cranium that protects the brain and the facial skeleton.

    23 structures · 1 topics · Head

    Vertebral column

    33 vertebrae with their discs: the flexible pillar that carries the head and protects the spinal cord.

    48 structures · 17 topics · Thorax

    Rib cage

    Twelve pairs of ribs, their costal cartilages and the sternum protect the heart and lungs and move with breathing.

    41 structures · 8 topics · Thorax

    Bony pelvis

    The two hip bones with the sacrum and coccyx transfer body weight to the legs and protect pelvic organs.

    3 structures · 15 topics · Pelvis

    Shoulder girdle

    Clavicle and scapula link each arm to the trunk.

    4 structures · 5 topics · Upper limb

    Hand skeleton

    27 bones per hand: 8 carpals, 5 metacarpals and 14 phalanges.

    54 structures · 22 topics · Upper limb

    Foot skeleton

    26 bones per foot: 7 tarsals, 5 metatarsals and 14 phalanges.

    52 structures · 24 topics · Lower limb

    Knee joint

    The largest joint in the body: femur, tibia and patella held by four ligaments and cushioned by two menisci, with the fibula alongside.

    10 structures · 2 topics · Lower limb

    Muscles

    Diaphragm

    The dome-shaped muscle separating chest from abdomen; the main muscle of breathing.

    1 structures · 9 topics · Thorax

    Heart

    Heart

    The four-chambered pump that drives blood around the body.

    20 structures · 96 topics · Thorax

    Arteries

    Coronary vessels

    The arteries and veins that supply and drain the heart muscle itself.

    26 structures · 31 topics · Thorax

    Aorta

    The largest artery, carrying blood from the left ventricle to the whole body.

    7 structures · 33 topics · Thorax

    Nervous system

    Brain

    The cerebrum, cerebellum and brainstem: about 1.4 kg of tissue that controls thought, movement, sensation and every organ.

    95 structures · 64 topics · Head

    Cerebellum

    The 'little brain' behind the brainstem coordinates balance, posture and the timing of movements.

    1 structures · 5 topics · Head

    Brainstem

    Midbrain, pons and medulla connect the brain to the spinal cord and run breathing, heart rate and consciousness.

    4 structures · 20 topics · Head

    Sensory organs

    Eye

    The eyeball and its parts: cornea, lens, iris, retina and the tissues that protect and move it.

    34 structures · 13 topics · Head

    Ear

    The external ear gathers sound; the middle and inner ear (not modelled here) convert it to nerve signals and sense balance.

    1 structures · 10 topics · Head

    Respiratory

    Nose

    Nasal bones and cartilages shape the nose; the conchae inside warm and filter the air.

    10 structures · 11 topics · Head

    Larynx

    The voice box: a cartilage frame with the vocal ligaments, guarding the airway and producing sound.

    40 structures · 17 topics · Neck

    Trachea & bronchial tree

    The windpipe and the branching airways that carry air into every segment of the lungs.

    25 structures · 21 topics · Thorax

    Lungs

    Two spongy organs of gas exchange. This dataset models the lungs through their segmental bronchial trees and pulmonary vessels rather than as solid lobes.

    81 structures · 62 topics · Thorax

    Digestive

    Tongue

    A muscular organ for taste, chewing, swallowing and speech.

    1 structures · 8 topics · Head

    Oesophagus

    A muscular tube carrying swallowed food from the pharynx to the stomach.

    1 structures · 14 topics · Thorax

    Stomach

    A muscular bag that churns food with acid and enzymes before releasing it into the duodenum.

    1 structures · 25 topics · Abdomen

    Small intestine

    Duodenum, jejunum and ileum: about six metres where most digestion and absorption happens.

    9 structures · 28 topics · Abdomen

    Large intestine

    Caecum, appendix, colon and rectum absorb water and form stool.

    11 structures · 23 topics · Abdomen

    Liver

    The body's chemical factory: it processes nutrients, detoxifies blood and makes bile. Modelled as eight hepatovenous segments with the biliary tree.

    22 structures · 72 topics · Abdomen

    Gallbladder & bile ducts

    Stores and concentrates bile, releasing it into the duodenum through the cystic and common bile ducts.

    14 structures · 10 topics · Abdomen

    Pancreas

    Makes digestive enzymes and the hormones insulin and glucagon.

    4 structures · 31 topics · Abdomen

    Urinary

    Kidneys

    Paired filters that clean the blood and regulate fluid and salts.

    2 structures · 78 topics · Abdomen

    Urinary bladder & urethra

    The bladder stores urine; the urethra carries it out of the body.

    2 structures · 18 topics · Pelvis

    Ureters

    Muscular tubes carrying urine from the kidneys to the bladder.

    4 structures · 6 topics · Abdomen

    Reproductive

    Prostate

    A walnut-sized gland below the bladder that adds fluid to semen.

    1 structures · 6 topics · Pelvis

    Testes & epididymis

    The testes produce sperm and testosterone; the epididymis stores maturing sperm.

    8 structures · 4 topics · Pelvis

    Penis

    Erectile bodies (corpora cavernosa and spongiosum) and the glans.

    3 structures · 1 topics · Pelvis

    Endocrine

    Adrenal glands

    Glands on top of each kidney producing cortisol, aldosterone and adrenaline.

    2 structures · 21 topics · Abdomen

    Pituitary gland

    The master gland at the base of the brain, controlling growth, thyroid, adrenal and reproductive hormones.

    1 structures · 22 topics · Head

    Lymphatic & immune

    Spleen

    Filters blood, recycles red cells and houses immune cells.

    1 structures · 20 topics · Abdomen

    Thymus

    Behind the sternum; trains T lymphocytes in childhood and shrinks with age.

    2 structures · 3 topics · Thorax
