    Rash

    Also known as: skin rash, spots, hives

    Any change in the colour or texture of the skin. Most rashes are harmless reactions, infections or inflammatory skin conditions; a few signal serious illness, especially when they come with fever, spread quickly or do not fade under pressure.

    Open in the 3D explorer

    What it is

    Any change in the colour or texture of the skin. Most rashes are harmless reactions, infections or inflammatory skin conditions; a few signal serious illness, especially when they come with fever, spread quickly or do not fade under pressure.

    Common causes

    Eczema (atopic dermatitis)Dry, itchy patches in the creases of elbows and knees.
    Contact dermatitis and irritantsRed itchy skin where something touched it: nickel, detergents, plants.
    Hives (urticaria)Itchy raised wheals that move around within hours, from allergy, infection or no clear cause.
    Viral rashesChickenpox, measles, slapped cheek, hand-foot-and-mouth; often with fever.
    Fungal infectionsRingworm, athlete's foot, thrush.
    PsoriasisWell-defined red plaques with silvery scale on elbows, knees and scalp.
    Acne, heat rash, insect bites

    Less common causes

    Meningococcal sepsisPurple spots or bruises that do not fade under a glass, with fever and being very unwell.
    Drug reactionsWidespread rash days after starting a medicine; blistering or peeling is an emergency.
    ShinglesA painful band of blisters on one side of the body.
    CellulitisSpreading, hot, tender redness.
    Autoimmune diseaseButterfly rash of lupus, the rash of dermatomyositis.
    Skin cancerA changing mole or a sore that will not heal.

    Often occurs with

    FeverSwelling (oedema)Joint painFatigue

    Seek urgent care if

    • A rash that does not fade when a glass is pressed on it, especially with fever or drowsiness
    • A rash with swelling of the face or tongue, wheeze or difficulty breathing
    • Blistering, peeling skin, or a rash affecting the mouth, eyes or genitals after a new medicine
    • A rapidly spreading hot, painful rash with fever
