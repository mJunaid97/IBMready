# SEO audit of the production build, 3 November 2026

Audited: v1.10.0 (commit 4005516), the build that went live that day. 836 HTML pages: 808 indexable and listed in the sitemaps, 27 `noindex`, the 404 page.
Everything here can be re-run: `python3 tools/qa/seo_audit.py dist` (page by page), `node tools/qa/smoke.mjs` (the assertions that fail a build),
Lighthouse against a server that compresses text the way production does. The sandbox cannot reach the live site or Search Console, so what happens in
Google's index is not measured here; the "Owner checklist" in `SEO.md` section 10 covers that.

## What was checked, and what was found

| Area | Method | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Titles and descriptions | every page: present, 15 to 65 and 70 to 160 characters, unique among indexable pages | no missing, no duplicates, none outside the limits. Weak spots fixed: hub titles read "[Name]: A–Z Guide"; the medicines hub description ended "doses."; medicine titles ended in "Mechanism" (searched about a hundredth as often as "side effects") and dropped "Side Effects" first when a name was long |
| Canonical, robots, sitemaps | canonical equals the page's own URL; sitemap URLs equal the indexable pages exactly | clean: 808 URLs in five child sitemaps, none `noindex`, no `priority` or `changefreq`, `lastmod` is each page's own update date |
| Language and locale | `<html lang>`, `inLanguage`, `og:locale` | **every page said `lang="en"`**; the content is British English. Now `en-GB`, `og:locale` `en_GB` |
| Snippet rules | robots value | plain `index,follow`. Now `index,follow,max-snippet:-1,max-image-preview:large,max-video-preview:-1`: large previews are allowed, and the 3D preview image is the reason to click |
| Stray pages | pages with no canonical, h1 or content | **`/blog/post.html`**, the query-URL template of the blog, was shipped (the other 16 templates are removed at build time; this one was missing from the list). Removed; the smoke test fails if any template is served |
| Structured data | every JSON-LD block parses; absolute URLs; Article, NewsArticle, BreadcrumbList, MedicalWebPage, Organization, WebSite, entity nodes | sound. One NewsArticle headline was 111 characters (Google cuts at 110): fixed, and the compiler now rejects a longer one. The explorer had no structured data: it now carries a `WebApplication` node |
| Open Graph, Twitter, feed | tags present and absolute | complete apart from `og:locale`; the RSS feed was announced only on the blog: now on every page of a production build |
| Internal links | link graph of the 808 pages | every page is within two clicks of the home page. **50 news briefs had two links in** (the hub and one related page): each post now ends with *More guides and news* and has at least three (asserted). 26 pages that are linked from every page are `noindex` (`/search/`, `/roadmap/`, one organ stub): harmless, left as they are |
| Home page links | what the home page links to | the new pages people look up most were not linked from the home page. *Popular conditions* and *Popular medicines* (twelve each) now are, and the hubs' "Start here" lists were re-chosen from search demand (below) |
| Server rules | `.htaccess`, `robots.txt` | HTTPS and one host in a single hop, caching, security headers, compression and a real 404 were already right. Added: a `.git` directory in the web root (the pull script makes one) is never served; Markdown and JSON files carry `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; `.webp` media type and caching |
| Thin pages | words of main content per indexable page | none under 150 words; 4 under 300 (the corrections, medical review, disclaimer and comparison pages); 69 under 500 (mostly the news briefs, counting everything in the main column). See "Decisions" |
| Lighthouse | seven templates, mobile, simulated slow 4G | accessibility 100, SEO 100, best practices 96 (the one deduction is the analytics tag, blocked in a sandbox) on every template; performance below |

## Speed

Measured with Lighthouse 12 (mobile profile: 4x slower CPU, 1.6 Mbps, 150 ms) against the production package. A local server that does not compress
text makes the 60 KB stylesheet and 100 KB of script six times as heavy as production serves them, so the numbers that matter are from a server that
compresses with Brotli.

| Page (compressed server) | Performance | Largest contentful paint | Layout shift |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Home | 99 | 2.0 s | 0.032 |
| Condition (depression) | 99 | 2.0 s | 0 |
| Medicine (semaglutide) | 99 | 2.0 s | 0 |
| Anatomy (aorta) | 99 | 1.9 s | 0 |
| Blog post | 98 | 2.3 s | 0.027 |

Tried and left out: **a preload for the Inter font.** With a compressing server it changed nothing that matters (LCP within 0.4 s either way; layout shift on the home page
fell from 0.03 to 0, both "good"), and on a server that does not compress it made the largest contentful paint up to 1.4 s later (1.2 to 1.4 s on three of seven pages), because the 73 KB
font competes with the render-blocking stylesheet. **Making the 3D preview image eager and high-priority** was kept: it is the largest element above the fold on
article pages and a lazy image there is the textbook cause of a late LCP, with no measurable cost. The home hero image is now WebP (80 KB, from 176 KB).

Not done: the stylesheet is one file of 60 KB of which about 45 KB is unused on any one page, and it blocks rendering. Splitting it per template would shorten
the first paint further; with compression it is 10 KB on the wire, so it is not urgent.

## Off the page (Ahrefs, 3 November 2026)

* Domain rating 0, no organic keywords, no organic traffic in the United Kingdom. The site is new and nothing has ranked yet; Ahrefs has crawled it
  (about, anatomy and the other sections are in its index).
* 406 backlinks from 405 referring domains, first seen 15 to 23 September 2026. They are automated "SEO services" spam: names such as
  `itxoft-professional-seo-services.site` and `buyseobacklinks.shop`, one link each, **401 of the 405 nofollow**. Four domains give a followed link (daxsupport.com,
  rating 25; kaila.biz, 14; link-pen.com, 6; wellness-care-shop.shop, 0). Nofollow links pass nothing and Google ignores spam links, so no action: no disavow
  file unless Search Console ever reports a manual action. Real links, which the site has none of, are the largest missing ranking factor.
* There is no Ahrefs Site Audit or Search Console project for this domain in the connected workspace (the projects listed belong to other sites).

## Decisions and risks, in order of weight

1. **Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools are the first step that only the owner can take.** Verify the domain, submit `https://anatomynexus.com/sitemap.xml`, and
   watch "Pages" for *Crawled, currently not indexed*. The site grew from about 450 to 808 indexable pages in one release; Google indexes a new domain slowly.
2. **The two biggest unanswered searches are anatomy pages with no article**: foot (about 90,000 a month across two phrasings, difficulty 0 to 4) and shoulder
   (45,000, difficulty 4 to 12), then rib cage, prostate, gallbladder and spleen (`docs/seo/keyword-research.md`). They are `noindex` until an article passes the two-pass
   source check (`CLINICAL.md` 5.5). This is the highest-value content still missing.
3. **75 news briefs of 110 to 270 words** are short by design (only checked sentences were kept) and summarise announcements whose originals rank above any copy.
   Google's guidance on mass-produced pages applies to pages made primarily to rank, which these are not, but a large share of short pages can lower how the whole site is judged.
   If Search Console shows most of them as *Crawled, currently not indexed* after a month, set them to `noindex` (a `seo.index` flag on each post) or fold them into a monthly digest
   page; do not add words for their own sake.
4. **Medical authority (E-E-A-T).** Health pages are judged on who stands behind them. Every page says it is source-verified and not clinically reviewed, which is honest, and the
   author is an organisation. A named clinician who reviews the pages (`review.reviewer` in the content and `reviewedBy` in the schema are already supported) would help more than any
   technical change.
5. **Links.** No real site links here. Likeliest sources: university and library anatomy resource lists, medical-student communities, the 3D explorer shared in teaching forums.
6. Left alone on purpose: the 26 `noindex` pages that every page links to; the Sitelinks search box markup (Google retired the feature, the markup is harmless); FAQ rich results
   (Google limits them to government and health authorities).
