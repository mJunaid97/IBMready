# Batch `news-global`: News briefs, international publishers

Read `docs/authoring/README.md` and `docs/authoring/blog.md` first. Branch to push to: `content/news-global`. Batch directory: `content/drafts/batches/news-global/`.

Goal: **as many verified news briefs as the search budget allows, up to 80**, each `"kind": "news"`, newest first, spread across publishers and
topics. Publishers: WHO and its regional offices (`who.int`, `afro.who.int`, `paho.org`), CDC (`cdc.gov`), FDA (`fda.gov`), NIH (`nih.gov`), EMA (`ema.europa.eu`), the UN and big public-health bodies. Window: 8 September to 3 November 2026.

Budget plan (200 searches): about 25 discovery searches (each returns several dated items), about 60 verification searches (one per item
that you keep, plus a few for items you drop), the rest spare. A brief that cannot be verified (the exact title search does not confirm the
date and the facts you state) is dropped. Link every brief to at least one existing page (a condition, medicine, test, symptom, physiology or
health topic id) so it is published; choose briefs the site can connect to.

Fragment: one `blog` entry per brief; the slug starts with the subject (`nice-recommends-enhertu-her2-low-breast-cancer`). Ledger and report per
brief (one file per brief is fine, or one file listing all briefs). `BATCH-REPORT.md` lists every brief with its publisher and date, and the items
you dropped and why.
