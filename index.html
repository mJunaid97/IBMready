Everyday and emergency knowledge

Anatomy Nexus is an educational platform built on public clinical guidance and a 3D atlas of one adult male reference body; the written anatomy pages cover female, male and gender-affirming anatomy. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Read the disclaimer and editorial policy.
