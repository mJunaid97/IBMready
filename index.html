    Anatomy Nexus is an educational platform built on public clinical guidance and a 3D atlas whose reference body is an adult male, with the female pelvic organs and breasts modelled from the NIH Human Reference Atlas and a schematic layer for the anatomy of gender-affirming surgery. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Read the disclaimer and editorial policy.
