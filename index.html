One connected body of knowledge

Every page is built from reusable entities with explicit links, so a reader can move naturally from a body part to its function, its conditions and the tests, procedures and treatments that follow.

This is an educational atlas of one adult male reference body. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not give medical advice.
