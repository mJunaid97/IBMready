Everyday and emergency knowledge

Anatomy Nexus is an educational platform built on public clinical guidance and a 3D atlas whose reference body is an adult male, with the female pelvic organs and breasts modelled from the NIH Human Reference Atlas and two schematic layers for the anatomy of gender-affirming surgery (vaginoplasty and phalloplasty). It is not a diagnostic tool and does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Read the disclaimer and editorial policy.
