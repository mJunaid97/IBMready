    Inhaled corticosteroids

    Also known as: preventer inhaler, brown inhaler, ICS, steroid inhaler

    The preventer inhalers of asthma: low doses of steroid delivered straight into the airways damp down inflammation so attacks become rarer and milder.

    What it is used for

    • The cornerstone of asthma treatment: daily to prevent attacks and control symptoms
    • COPD with frequent exacerbations and high blood eosinophils, combined with long-acting bronchodilators
    • Combined inhalers with formoterol used as both preventer and reliever (MART regimens)

    How it works in the body

    Steroid molecules deposited on the airway lining diffuse into cells and bind glucocorticoid receptors, which move to the nucleus and switch off the genes for inflammatory cytokines, reduce eosinophils and mast cells, and shrink mucosal swelling and mucus. Inhaling delivers a tiny dose straight to the airways, so the whole-body effects of steroid tablets are largely avoided. Benefit builds over days to weeks and depends on daily use.

    Common side effects

    • Oral thrush and hoarse voice (rinse the mouth and use a spacer)
    • Sore throat, cough on inhaling

    Serious: seek help

    • Pneumonia risk in COPD at higher doses
    • At high doses long term: adrenal suppression, reduced bone density, cataract and glaucoma, easy bruising, a small effect on growth in children
    • Sudden stopping of high doses can unmask adrenal insufficiency

    Cautions and interactions

    • Use the lowest dose that keeps control, stepped up or down at review
    • Long-acting beta agonists must never be used alone in asthma without a steroid
    • Carry a steroid card at high doses; check bone density if on high dose for years
    • Technique check at every review

    Metformin + Corticosteroids (class)

    Severity not gradedInteraction documented

    Systemic corticosteroids raise blood glucose and can reduce diabetes control on metformin.

    Why it can occur
    pharmacodynamic antagonism
    What official information says
    UK information lists steroid tablets among medicines that affect blood sugar.
    What to discuss with a clinician
    Documented; glucose monitoring during and after a steroid course.
    What may be monitored
    Blood glucose · Blood glucose
