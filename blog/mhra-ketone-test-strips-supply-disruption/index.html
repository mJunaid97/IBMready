    MHRA alert on ketone test strip supply

    Health news Published 9 October 2026 · sources checked 3 November 2026

    The MHRA warned of disruption to blood ketone testing after Nova Biomedical recalled StatStrip ketone test strip lots and paused distribution.

    Key points

    • The MHRA issued a Device Safety Information notice on 9 October 2026 about Nova Biomedical StatStrip ketone test strips.
    • Nova Biomedical has recalled specified lots after repeated quality-control failures and paused distribution of those lots.
    • The company says it cannot replenish UK stock for roughly four weeks.
    • The MHRA says disruption to ketone testing risks delayed recognition or exclusion of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA).

    What was announced

    The MHRA says Nova Biomedical has recalled specified lots of StatStrip ketone test strips after repeated quality-control failures and has paused distribution of those lots. Any further lots that fail quality control are to be withdrawn, which could steadily reduce the strips available for clinical use. The company has said it cannot replenish UK stock for about four weeks.

    What it means

    The MHRA warns that disruption to ketone testing risks delayed recognition or exclusion of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA). DKA is a serious complication of diabetes.

    Where supply is limited, the MHRA advises clinicians to prioritise testing for patients at greatest risk, including those with suspected or established DKA, children, and acutely unwell people with type 1 diabetes.

    Source Nova Biomedical StatStrip ketone test strips: risk of disruption to blood ketone testing due to quality-control failures and supply interruption DSI/2026/013 · MHRA · 9 October 2026
