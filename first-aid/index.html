    First aid

    First aid and emergency guidance written to follow the Resuscitation Council UK 2021 guidelines, the European Resuscitation Council, the International Liaison Committee on Resuscitation consensus, and NHS and St John Ambulance public advice. It is educational and no substitute for a certified first-aid course. 'Emergency services' means 999 in the UK, 112 across Europe and 911 in North America.
