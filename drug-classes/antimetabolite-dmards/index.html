    Antimetabolite DMARDs

    Antimetabolite DMARDs are a group of disease-modifying medicines; methotrexate is the member covered on this site. Methotrexate is an immunosuppressant used for inflammatory conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis and Crohn's disease, and sometimes for cancer. The NHS says it calms the immune system and helps reduce inflammation.

    Open in the 3D explorerLiver anatomy

    Mechanism of action

    Methotrexate blocks an enzyme called dihydrofolate reductase, which the body uses to make tetrahydrofolate, a form of folate needed to build DNA and RNA. In inflammatory conditions, how low-dose methotrexate works is less clear.

    Cautions

    • Taking it daily by mistake instead of weekly has caused overdoses that were sometimes fatal.
    • The MHRA introduced new measures in 2020 to reduce this risk, including a patient card for oral products.
    • Regular blood tests are used to monitor treatment.
    • The NHS says methotrexate is not recommended in pregnancy because it can cause birth defects.
