    Triptans

    Also known as: triptan, 5-HT1 agonists for migraine

    Triptans are medicines for treating migraine attacks, and sumatriptan is the triptan covered on this site. The NHS says sumatriptan relieves symptoms of migraine but does not prevent migraines from starting.

    Open in the 3D explorerBrain anatomy
    Also called
    triptan, 5-HT1 agonists for migraine

    Mechanism of action

    The NHS says migraines may be linked to temporarily widened blood vessels in the brain, and that sumatriptan narrows these blood vessels.

    Conditions treated

    Migraine

    Cautions

    • Heart problems, a past stroke or transient ischaemic attack, circulation problems in the legs and liver disease are listed by the NHS as reasons sumatriptan may not be suitable.
    • Combining sumatriptan with some antidepressants or St John's wort can raise the risk of serotonin syndrome, according to the NHS and UK leaflets.
