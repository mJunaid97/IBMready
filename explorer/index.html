          How to use the atlas

          Navigate

          • Drag to orbit · Right-drag or two-finger drag to pan · Scroll / pinch to zoom
          • Double-click a structure to fly to it

          Inspect

          • Click a structure to open its card; Shift-click to add to the selection
          • Isolate shows only the selection; Hide removes it from view

          Keyboard

          • / search · Esc close / clear
          • 1–5 views · R reset · X x-ray
          • H hide selection · I isolate · F focus
          • E/Shift+E explode more / less · Q study mode (identify, locate, flashcards) · P screenshot

          Performance


          Phones get the light model automatically. Changing this reloads the page.
